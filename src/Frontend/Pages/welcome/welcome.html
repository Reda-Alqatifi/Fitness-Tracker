About

Fitness Tracker is a web application built for anyone who wants to take real control of their health. Getting fit rarely fails for lack of motivation. It fails because progress is hard to see, advice is scattered across a dozen apps and notebooks, and it is easy to lose track of where you started. Fitness Tracker brings everything into one place. You tell us about yourself (your birth date, height, weight, gender, activity level, and whether you want to lose, gain, or maintain weight), and the platform turns those numbers into a clear, personal picture of your fitness that grows with you every day.

As soon as you sign in, your dashboard shows the numbers that matter most: your weight, your Body Mass Index (BMI), your Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM), your Percent Body Fat (PBF), and the exercises planned for today. You don't have to dig through menus or work anything out by hand. Every main section is one click away from a clean, consistent navigation bar: Reports, Exercises, Diet, and Profile. Whether you check in for thirty seconds before work or sit down to plan your whole week, the answer you need is right in front of you.

Our Reports page turns your daily effort into visible progress. It sums up your current body status (weight, height, BMI, SMM, PBF, and Body Fat Mass) in a single card, then goes further with simple, easy-to-read graphs. You can compare the calories you ate today with the calories you burned, follow the calories you burned on each day of the current month, keep an eye on your daily step count, and watch your weight change over any period you choose. When you want to share your progress with a coach or doctor, or just keep a record for yourself, one click exports the full report as a PDF file.

Nutrition and training are the two pillars of any fitness journey, and Fitness Tracker supports both. On the Diet page, you can start from a recommended diet built around your goal or design a custom one from scratch. Add, edit, or remove meals whenever you like, with each meal's protein, carbohydrates, fats, and calories recorded. Progress bars show how your intake compares to your recommended macronutrients and your daily calorie target. On the Exercises page, you can follow a recommended starter schedule or build your own, choose which days to train each exercise, log the weight you lift in kilograms or pounds, and get an estimate of the calories you burn based on your workout volume.

We designed Fitness Tracker to feel like yours. It is fully available in both English and Arabic, so you can switch languages at any time. It adapts smoothly to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens, so your progress goes wherever you go. Your Profile page keeps your personal details up to date, from your name, age, and height to your target weight, goal, and activity level. It also shows the date you joined, so you can look back on how far you've come. When your circumstances change, your plan changes with them.

Behind the friendly interface is a platform built with care for your trust and your data. Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are protected with secure hashing. Every account is fully isolated, so you and only you can see or change your personal and fitness information. All input is checked before it is saved, errors are explained in plain language, and your history stays safe between sessions for as long as you want to keep it. You stay in charge the whole time: you can change your password or permanently delete your account whenever you choose.

Fitness Tracker was designed and built by Group (1) as part of the Fundamentals of Web Programming course at the College of Computer Sciences & Information Technology, King Faisal University. We built it with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend and PHP and MySQL on the backend, but more than a technical project, it reflects a simple belief: understanding your body is the first step to changing it. Whatever your goal is, Fitness Tracker is here to help you measure it, follow it, and reach it. Your fitness, your health, all in one place.
